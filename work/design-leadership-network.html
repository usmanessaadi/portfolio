Redesigning the website and member experience for a 19-year-old design community.

A new marketing website and member dashboard giving architecture and interior-design leaders a clearer way to discover, join, and engage with the network.

Overview

Design Leadership Network is a professional membership organisation serving principals and leaders of architecture, interior design, landscape architecture, and related creative fields across North America. For 19 years, it has built community through events, resources, collaboration, and peer connection in the high-end design industry.

The engagement combined a full marketing-website redesign with a new member dashboard. Prospective members and partners needed to understand the value of the network, while existing members needed one place to discover content, manage their profiles, find peers, and participate in events.

The challenge

The existing WordPress experience was slow and fragmented. Event registration relied on separate CVENT microsites, the member portal lived outside the main website, content was difficult to search, and the experience did not translate consistently to mobile.

DLN already had a strong brand identity, but its digital presence did not communicate the credibility, inspiration, connection, and longevity behind the organisation. The new experience needed to feel premium and intentional without making an established members-only community feel unwelcoming.

Finding the right visual direction took several rounds. The client reviewed multiple distinct approaches before we reached a direction everyone was confident in, which then became the foundation for the full build.

Approach

Working within the Arch Web Design team, I led the visual design of the marketing website and designed the member-dashboard experience in parallel. I worked within DLN’s existing brand guide, presented the work directly to the client, and refined both experiences through feedback.

The website introduced clearer routes for events, membership, resources, the directory, and DLN’s story. The interface was also designed around planned Mailchimp, MemberClicks, and Authorize.net integrations supporting communications, membership management, and payments; implementation of those systems was outside my design scope.

Member dashboard

The dashboard carried the more complex product scope, bringing member activity and content into a single responsive experience.

Membership

Home dashboard, navigation, profile management, sign-in, password recovery, and onboarding states.

People and partners

Member directory in list and grid views, partner directory, and detailed member and partner profiles.

Events

All events, personal events, individual event pages, registration pathways, and calendar views.

Content library

Resources, bookmarks, and dedicated experiences for audio, video, and document content.

What shipped

The final design delivery covered the complete responsive marketing website and a member dashboard spanning more than 20 desktop and mobile screens.

Outcome

The redesigned website launched with a clearer expression of DLN’s membership, events, resources, and long-standing community. Together, the website and dashboard established one coherent digital home designed to reduce fragmentation and make it easier for members to discover content, connect with peers, and engage with events.
